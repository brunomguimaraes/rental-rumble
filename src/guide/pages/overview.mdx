You are a Trainer. Professor Andre, a stone and fossil researcher, offers you three weak, non-fossil starters and you keep
one. Battles play out turn by turn with no inputs. These pages
document what happens under the hood — the stats, the signs and the move logic —
so nothing about a result is a mystery.

## Travel

**Travel.** Every trip between places costs travel stamina: 4 on foot. A Pokémon in your party that can carry you makes it cheaper — 2 on a land mount like Rapidash, 1 on a flyer like Pidgeot. Travel refills one point every 15 minutes, up to 12. Run out away from town and you stay where you are until it refills. Searching needs actions, and you can only search Sunny Meadow while you're there.

## Exploring Sunny Meadow

Open the world map to visit Hearth Town or Route 1 · Sunny Meadow. The meadow
offers **Find wild Pokémon**, **Find NPC**, and **Explore**. Each search costs one
action. Your allowance starts at 12, recovers one action every ten minutes, and
holds up to 48. Finishing an encounter costs no additional actions. Time away
refills the allowance but never battles or earns rewards for you.

Wild Pokémon can be battled, caught, or left alone. Battles resolve automatically;
you can advance or skip their replay. Winning gives your starting party EXP and
improves your catch chance. A wild encounter permits one throw, before its battle
or after a win. A failed throw or a lost battle ends that encounter.

## Health and the Pokémon Center

Pokémon keep the HP they finish a battle with. A Pokémon at 0 HP has fainted: it
stays in your party but sits out battles and earns no EXP. Only party members
still standing when a battle is won earn its EXP. If your whole party faints, you
hurry back to Hearth Town, and you can't search Sunny Meadow until someone can
fight again. Visit the Pokémon Center in Hearth Town to heal every Pokémon you
own, party and Box, instantly and for free. HP does not recover anywhere else.

## Your Bag

Your Bag holds Poké Balls and Great Balls. You receive 20 Poké Balls when you first
begin exploring. Every throw consumes one owned ball, whether it succeeds or fails.
Great Balls improve the catch chance; the catch screen shows the exact chance
before you throw. Looking in the Bag or cancelling a selection consumes nothing.

Explore can find more balls. With no balls remaining, your next Explore search
guarantees three Poké Balls. You can still battle or leave an encounter with an
empty Bag. Finish or leave the current encounter before searching for supplies.

## People and landmarks

Meet trainers for optional battles, or a researcher who asks you to discover the
Old Signpost, Sunflower Patch, and Hilltop Oak. Explore discovers these landmarks
alongside Pokémon, NPCs, or items. After accepting the survey and finding all three,
claim three Great Balls from the route panel. The quest has no deadline and its
reward can be claimed once. Previously found landmarks count.

The map preserves your view when you return. Unfinished encounters resume after
a reload, and rewards are saved as soon as their outcome resolves. There are no
guardian gates or idle training sessions. Town services will arrive separately.
